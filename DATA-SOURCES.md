# Data Provenance, Methodology & Privacy Policy

Foundation Explorer is a business-to-business (B2B) intelligence platform
engineered to provide evidence-backed donor prospecting shortlists for
faith-aligned non-profit organizations. We maintain a strict commitment to
data transparency, legal compliance, and user privacy.

### 1. Core Data Sourcing

Our underlying dataset is compiled entirely from public-domain records
mandated by the United States Federal Government.

- **The Foundation Universe:** Established natively using the official
  Internal Revenue Service (IRS) Exempt Organization Business Master File
  (EO BMF).
- **Filing & Transactional Data:** Programmatically extracted from
  machine-readable Form 990-PF tax returns hosted in the public IRS Tax
  Exempt and Government Entities (TEOS) XML archives.

### 2. Proprietary Synthesis & Analytics

While the underlying raw tax facts reside in the public domain, the specific
coordination, classification algorithms, faith-alignment scoring models, and
user-interface presentation are the exclusive proprietary property of
Foundation Explorer.

- Our alignment metrics are derived through a combination of deterministic
  rule-based engines and localized algorithmic analysis.
- **Pro-Tip & Disclaimer:** All prospecting scores, tags, and metrics are
  analytical estimations designed to assist fundraising teams and should be
  independently verified by your organization prior to donor outreach.

### 3. Third-Party Platform Boundaries

Foundation Explorer operates completely independently of third-party
non-profit aggregators.

- **No API Dependencies:** We do not interface with, pull data from, or
  scrape ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer or any similar platform.
- **Outbound Citations:** For user convenience and verification, our
  dashboard provides outbound hyperlinks directly to public organization
  profiles on ProPublica. These links are standard navigational shortcuts to
  assist you in viewing original PDF source filings and do not constitute an
  ingestion pipeline.

### 4. Zero-Leak AI & Data Privacy Boundary

We take data security seriously. Unlike legacy tools that rely on
commercial, third-party AI APIs, Foundation Explorer handles all advanced
processing locally.

- **Local Architecture:** Our classification loops run entirely inside our
  secure, self-hosted infrastructure using open-source, localized language
  models.
- **Data Guarantee:** Your search queries, donor shortlists, and platform
  interactions are never transmitted to external commercial AI vendors for
  processing or model training. Your operational intelligence stays entirely
  within our secure boundary.
